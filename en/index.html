FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer a 24/7 emergency electrician in Bodrum?

Yes. We provide day and night emergency electrical repair across Bodrum Center, Yalikavak, Turgutreis, Gundogan, Bitez and the entire Bodrum peninsula — including weekends and holidays. Call or WhatsApp us any time.

Do you supply the materials?

No, we don't sell materials. You purchase the products (chandeliers, fixtures, smart home devices etc.) and we take care of the installation and wiring. We're happy to advise you for free on what to buy beforehand.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover all of Bodrum including Bodrum Center, Yalikavak, Gundogan, Golturkbuku, Golkoy, Torba, Turgutreis, Gumusluk, Bitez, Ortakent, Yahsi, Konacik, Gumbet, Akyarlar, Kadikalesi, Guvercinlik, Yakakoy and Mumcular.

Can you install a smart home without breaking walls?

Yes. Using wireless (Zigbee / Wi‑Fi) smart switches, sockets, blind motors and sensors we upgrade your home without touching the existing wiring. For new builds we recommend a wired KNX system.

How long does a smart home installation take?

A wireless smart home setup for an apartment usually takes 1 day. Comprehensive villa automation projects are installed within 2–7 days.

Do you install heavy chandeliers on high ceilings?

Yes. We safely mount crystal and heavy chandeliers with proper anchoring — using scaffolding when needed — up to 6–8 metres high, including dimmer and smart control connections.

When should electrical wiring be replaced?

If breakers trip often, sockets get hot or smell burnt, the wiring is older than 20 years, or there is no RCD (residual current device), your installation should be inspected and renewed.

Is the site visit and quote free?

Site visits and quotes are free for projects within Bodrum. For emergency repairs, the price is clearly stated before any work begins.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. All our installation work and workmanship is guaranteed. We also offer after‑installation support and maintenance contracts.

Do you speak English and Russian?

Yes. We work with many international villa owners and property managers in Bodrum and communicate in English and Russian, with quotes and invoices in your preferred language.
